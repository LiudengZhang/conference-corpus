# AACR IO 2026 — Program

Day-by-day program for [AACR IO 2026](index.md), February 18–21, 2026, JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. Live.

!!! danger "Reconstructed from Wayback snapshots"
    The live AACR program pages return **HTTP 404**. Everything below comes from archived snapshots, listed at the bottom of this page. **The Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday snapshots date from January 13–20, 2026 — before the meeting** — and still contain "Additional speaker to be announced" placeholders and unnamed short-talk slots. The **Saturday snapshot (February 8)** is the most complete, including room assignments and named short talks. A final as-delivered program was never archived.

**Format**, in AACR's words: "a mix of keynote lectures, major symposia, spotlight sessions, educational sessions, special sessions, and poster sessions with exhibits." Note that **no special session appears in any archived 2026 program** despite the boilerplate.

!!! tip "Friday afternoon is the day to read"
    MS7 and MS8 running in parallel is the meeting's most distinctive hour. MS7 is a full symposium on in vivo T-cell engineering staffed almost entirely by company speakers; MS8 is neuro-psych-immunology, a theme that barely exists at other IO meetings. SS4 immediately after, with Nolan and Linghua Wang, is the single-cell and spatial slot — see [Themes](themes.md#spatial-single-cell-and-systems-immunology-18-abstracts).
