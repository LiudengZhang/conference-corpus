# AACR IO 2026 — Notable readouts

Clinical and translational data presented at [AACR IO 2026](index.md). Thirty-five late-breaking and clinical-trial abstracts were published (DOIs dated 2026-03-18); these are the ones with the most substance behind them.

All abstracts follow the DOI pattern `10.1158/2326-6074.io2026-<id>`.

## OBX-115 — engineered TIL in advanced melanoma

**The best-documented readout of the meeting**, and the only one with a full public poster PDF.

- **Abstract LB-A010** — *"Translational data validate OBX-115 mechanism of action: Impact of dosing on clinical outcome in advanced (adv) melanoma"* · DOI [`10.1158/2326-6074.io2026-lb-a010`](https://doi.org/10.1158/2326-6074.io2026-lb-a010)
- **Trial:** Agni-01, single-arm Phase 1/2, [NCT06060613](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06060613)
- **Sponsor:** Obsidian Therapeutics
- **Presenting author:** Gino K. In (USC Norris); senior author Jason A. Chesney (UofL Brown Cancer Center); co-authors include Shoushtari (MSKCC), Amaria (MD Anderson), Betof (Stanford), Moyers (The Angeles Clinic)

**Mechanism — the reason this one matters.** OBX-115 is a tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte product expressing **membrane-bound IL-15 regulated by acetazolamide**, an already-approved small molecule. The regulated mbIL15 **abrogates the need for systemic IL-2**, which is the toxicity that has kept TIL therapy confined to centers able to manage it. Removing IL-2 widens eligibility more than any efficacy increment would.

**Data reported:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Total treated | **11** (DL1 n=3, DL2 n=2, DL3/RP2D n=6) |
| ORR at DL3 / RP2D | **67% (4/6)** |
| Complete responses at DL3 / RP2D | **2 (33%)** |
| ctDNA | eVAF declined by Day 14 in all evaluable patients |
| PD-1 expression | <1% on peripheral CD8+ and CD3+CD56+ cells through Day 56 |

**Read the denominators before the response rate.** Six patients at the recommended Phase 2 dose is hypothesis-generating, not practice-changing. The mechanism and the ctDNA kinetics are the durable contributions here.

**Poster PDF (company-hosted, open):** <https://obsidiantx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/AACR-IO-2026_Agni-01-Translational_2026-02-10_FINAL.pdf>

*OncLive covered this as "OBX-115 With Acetazolamide Induces Safe and Durable Antitumor Responses in Advanced Melanoma," but the article is Cloudflare-protected and its body text is unverified here.*

Related: **Parameswaran Hari** of Obsidian Therapeutics spoke in MS6 (TILs and Maintenance of Immunity).

## Obrixtamig (BI 764532) — DLL3×CD3 T-cell engager

**Five abstracts — the largest single-program block at the meeting.** Boehringer Ingelheim brought a coordinated data package rather than a single readout.

| Abstract | Content |
|---|---|
| **LB-A001** | R/R DLL3-high extrapulmonary neuroendocrine carcinoma |
| **LB-A003** | Efficacy and safety in EP-NEC with **high *or low*** DLL3 expression *(presenting author reported as Tim Remus, Boehringer Ingelheim — attribution from a search snippet, not primary-verified)* |
| **LB-A004** | **DAREON-7** — Phase I dose escalation/expansion, first-line obrixtamig + chemotherapy |
| **LB-A005** | **DAREON-8** — Phase I, first-line obrixtamig + chemotherapy + atezolizumab in ES-SCLC |
| **LB-A002** | Separate BI asset: **BI 765179** (CD137/FAP agonist) + pembrolizumab, open-label Phase Ib dose expansion, first line |

The strategically interesting one is **LB-A003**: extending into *low* DLL3 expressers is an argument that the target threshold is lower than assumed, which would materially expand the addressable population. Watch whether it survives a randomized setting.

## CART19/20 — bispecific CAR-T in non-Hodgkin lymphoma

- **Abstract LB-C001** — *"Phase 1 Trial of Bispecific CART19/20 Cells for Relapsed or Refractory Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma: Updated Results with Over Four Years Median Follow Up"* (UCLA)
- Presenting author reported as Sophie Carlson (UCLA) — *attribution from a search snippet, not primary-verified*

**"Over four years median follow-up" is the finding.** Durability data at that horizon in a Phase 1 CAR-T cohort is rare and is worth more than most first-report response rates. The bispecific CD19/CD20 design directly targets antigen-escape relapse, and four years is long enough to say something real about whether it works.

## NIBIT-ML1 — epigenetic reprogramming to overcome PD-1 resistance

- **Abstract LB-C004** — *"Epigenetic immune reprogramming overcomes PD-1 resistance in metastatic melanoma patients: the phase II NIBIT-ML1 study"* (University of Siena / NIBIT, Italy); reported presenter Anna Maria Di Giacomo
- Companion: **LB-C009** — NIBIT-EPI-MESO, methylation subtypes predicting IO outcome in pleural mesothelioma

**Cross-meeting note:** the same study was re-presented at the **AACR Annual Meeting 2026 as abstract CT236** (*Cancer Research* vol 86, 8_Supplement). This is one of the few cases where the corpus can follow a single study across two meetings two months apart — a useful test case for how presentation framing shifts between an IO-specialist audience and a general oncology one. See [`aacr-2026/topics/clinical-trials/`](../aacr-2026/topics/clinical-trials/index.md).

## A clean negative result — chronotherapy in adjuvant melanoma

- **Abstract LB-B005** — adjuvant pembrolizumab, NCI/SWOG resectable melanoma: **"No effect of the time-of-day infusion"** on outcomes.

Worth flagging on its own. Time-of-day effects on immunotherapy have generated a run of positive retrospective reports; a null result from a cooperative-group trial population is the kind of finding that rarely gets amplified and should be. File alongside the negative-result material in [PSB 2026](../psb-2026/themes.md#foundation-models-and-llms-the-dominant-theme) as evidence the corpus tracks refutations, not just claims.

## The autoimmunity readout — CAR-Tregs in rheumatoid arthritis

Presented in Fred Ramsdell's closing-day keynote rather than as an abstract. Phase I, 9 patients with severe refractory RA, no adverse events reported, synovial inflammation reduced by week four. Full detail on the [keynotes page](keynotes.md#fred-ramsdell).

## Caveats

- **Presenter attributions for LB-C001 and LB-A003** came from search snippets rather than primary reads of the abstracts. Verify against Crossref before citing.
- **OncLive and BioWorld coverage is paywalled or Cloudflare-blocked**; no trade-press body text was read for this page.
- **No AACR press-program release exists** for this meeting — unusual for an AACR conference, and it means there is no official curated "top abstracts" list to check this selection against.
