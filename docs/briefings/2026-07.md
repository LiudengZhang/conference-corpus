## 1. Foundation models: the premise broke while the products shipped

**Seven independent papers, four journals, one month.**

| PMID | Journal | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **[42265212](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42265212/)** | *Nature Methods* | Scaling transcriptomic AI training data is **"much pain with little gain"** |
| **[42265208](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42265208/)** | *Nature Methods* | Companion: pretraining dataset size and diversity **do not buy** single-cell FM performance |
| [42443524](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42443524/) | *Nature Computational Science* | Larger protein language models do **not** monotonically improve fitness prediction |
| [42475685](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42475685/) | *Nucleic Acids Research* | Systematic contextual bias in SegmentNT and other nucleotide transformers invalidates naive benchmarks |
| [42412799](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42412799/) | *Bioinformatics* | Standard train/test splits **leak sequence composition** and inflate reported accuracy |
| [42260311](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42260311/) | *Nature Methods* | Sequence-to-function models tested against real inter-individual variation (Mostafavi) |
| [42521815](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42521815/) | *Mol Systems Biology* | Genomic FMs fail on individual-level expression prediction until re-decoded |

→ `ev-2026-07-scfm-scaling-null`

These are not scattered negative results. They converge on one claim: **the scaling hypothesis, imported from language modelling, does not hold for biological sequence and expression data**, and a good part of the reported progress is measurement artifact.

A second, harder argument arrived alongside it. A cluster in *Cell Systems* and *Molecular Systems Biology* (42242211, [42462718](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42462718/), and three companions) shows single-cell response is set by **heritable non-genetic state and prior exposure history** rather than genotype or noise. If a cell's response is a function of an exposure history you did not observe, expression state is an insufficient input — and no amount of additional data fixes that. → `ev-2026-07-cellular-memory-ceiling`

### And in the same month, the clinical models shipped

- **Compass** (*Nature Medicine*, 07-03) — pan-cancer immunotherapy-response prediction from bulk transcriptomes through a **concept-bottleneck transformer** with 44 biologically grounded immune concepts. Marinka Žitnik, last author.
- **PRISM2** (*Nature Medicine*, 07-31, PMID 42538427) — slide-level pathology FM: 2.3M whole-slide images, 14M question-answer pairs derived from 700K pathology reports.
- **CLEAR** (*Nature Biomedical Engineering*, PMID 42486902) — a radiology FM deliberately grounded in clinical concepts **for regulatory auditability**.

→ `ev-2026-07-clinical-fm-shipped`

**Read those two facts together, because that is the finding.** Application is running ahead of its methodological foundation. And note what the three shipped models have in common: Compass trades capacity for 44 inspectable concepts, CLEAR is built around auditability. The clinical layer is moving *away* from scale-first exactly as the scale-first program is being refuted underneath it.

One more refutation on the same thread: *Nature Medicine* 07 (PMID 42286322) reports that **general-purpose LLMs outperform specialized clinical AI tools**, which undermines the economic case for building bespoke clinical models at all. → `ev-2026-07-general-llm-beats-specialist`

**Thread status moved `forming` → `crisis`.** This is the corpus's most heavily invested thread — the entire [Foundation Models briefing](../foundation-models.md) and the [FMs to Virtual Cells](../talks/fm-to-virtual-cells.md) material sit on it. Those pages now need a July addendum, not a rewrite: the clinical claims survive, the scaling claims do not.

## 2. Three threads were one thread

In February, AACR IO 2026 gave a full minisymposium (**MS8, Neuro-Psych-Immunology**) to neurotransmitters as tumor-immunity modulators, and this corpus recorded it as *"the most distinctive programming at the meeting, and not keyword-countable because it barely has a literature yet"* — [AACR IO 2026 → Themes](../conferences/aacr-io-2026/themes.md#neuro-psych-immunology-ms8).

July produced **thirteen papers**, and — more importantly — the bridges.

**The paper that forced the merge:** *Cell*, PMID **[42161272](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42161272/)** — **nociceptive innervation actively suppresses tertiary lymphoid structure formation** to promote lung cancer. Sensory nerves are not adjacent to TLS biology; they are upstream of it, and nerve blockade becomes a TLS-enabling strategy. → `ev-2026-07-nociceptive-tls`

**The second bridge:** *Nature*, PMID **[42236946](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42236946/)** — **commensal bacteria synthesize acetylcholine** and use it to instruct mucosal immunity. The microbiome does not merely modulate immunity in parallel with the nervous system; it manufactures the same signalling molecules. → `ev-2026-07-commensal-acetylcholine`

The rest of the network:

| PMID / DOI | Journal | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| [10.1016/j.ccell.2026.06.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ccell.2026.06.006) | *Cancer Cell* | **GABA promotes resistance to immunotherapy in TLS-positive tumors** — Wolf H. Fridman, last author |
| **[42337051](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42337051/)** / **[42337052](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42337052/)** | *Nature Cancer* | GABAergic signalling shapes glioblastoma immunity **sex-dependently**, acting through MDSCs in females |
| **[42349430](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42349430/)** | *Cancer Cell* | Chronic psychological stress unleashes an intratumoral **bacteriophage–fibroblast–B cell circuit** |
| [10.1126/science.ady1678](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.ady1678) | *Science* | CCR7⁺ mature DCs build and maintain TLS; early development requires IFN-γ–driven cDC1 maturation — Miriam Merad |
| [10.1016/j.ccell.2026.06.022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ccell.2026.06.022) | *Cancer Cell* | Antibiotic-associated *Enterocloster* deplete secondary bile acids; **subclinical cholestasis** is the readable hallmark of dysbiosis-driven ICI resistance — Laurence Zitvogel |
| **[42485431](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42485431/)** | *Sci Transl Med* | **Blocking the VIP–VIPR neuropeptide axis reprograms CAR T cells** for superior efficacy |
| **[42525787](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42525787/)** | *Sci Transl Med* | Cancer-induced nerve injury reveals a sympathetic-to-sensory axis in head and neck cancer |

**The last row is where this stops being descriptive biology.** VIP–VIPR blockade is a cell-therapy engineering parameter. Six months after a conference programmed neurotransmitters as a curiosity, they are a CAR-T manufacturing variable.

`neuro-immuno-oncology`, `tls-as-target`, and `microbiome-io` are now aliases of **`neuro-immune-microbiome-axis`**. Old ids resolve; nothing breaks.

The corollary from the February vault still stands and is now harder to dismiss: if neurotransmitters modulate immunotherapy response, **commonly prescribed psychiatric drugs are a confounder in every IO trial ever run.** July did not test it. July made it expensive to ignore.

### The microbiome mismatch, resolved in the organizers' favor

This corpus flagged that AACR IO 2026 gave microbiome a **full educational session and received 2 abstracts** — [the programmed-versus-submitted mismatch](../conferences/aacr-io-2026/themes.md#the-programmed-versus-submitted-mismatch). July: Zitvogel in *Cancer Cell*, a prospective **FMT rechallenge trial in ICI-resistant solid cancers** (MITRIC, PMID 42476727), and *Blautia coccoides*-derived acetate named as a causal, druggable anti-PD-1 adjuvant (PMID 42463281).

**The organizers were early, not wrong.** That is a scored point for reading programs as forward-looking bets.

## 4. Neoantigens split into two arguments

The clinical and computational arms of this thread moved in **opposite directions** in the same month.

**Advancing:**

- *Cancer Discovery* 07-16 — phase I first-in-human peptide vaccine against six common KRAS mutations (**mKRAS-VAX**), given to **20 individuals with hereditary pancreatic cancer predisposition and a radiographic finding** — not to patients with cancer ([10.1158/2159-8290.cd-25-2245](https://doi.org/10.1158/2159-8290.cd-25-2245)). **Elizabeth M. Jaffee**, who gave the February AACR IO keynote *"The Cancer Vaccine Era Has Arrived"*, is a co-author.
- *Nature Cancer* (PMID 42120910) — adjuvant personalized multivalent neoantigen DNA vaccine in MGMT-unmethylated glioblastoma.
- *J Immunother Cancer* (PMID 42481156) — neoantigen-encoding tumor-specific transcripts reveal a **shared** target reservoir in hepatocellular carcinoma, which would make off-the-shelf vaccines feasible.

**Refuting:**

- *Cell Genomics* (PMID **[41985452](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41985452/)**) — cross-task interpretability shows neoantigen and immunogenicity prediction models **exploit a shared shortcut feature rather than learning biology**. → `ev-2026-07-neoantigen-shortcut-bias`
- *Immunity* (PMID **[42276071](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42276071/)**) — **immunoediting already restricts clonal neoantigens in primary, treatment-naive human tumors.** The target space is smaller than assumed before any therapy is given. → `ev-2026-07-immunoediting-clonal-neoantigens`

One attacks the prediction method, the other attacks the target space itself, and they are independent. Meanwhile first-in-human trials are enrolling.

**Status `splitting`.** The thread cannot be summarized in one direction and should not be. This is exactly the state a `stance` field exists to represent.

## 5. Agentic AI got benchmarks

The three-point timeline is now complete: **PSB 2026** (January — four peer-reviewed papers across three sessions) → **GCC2026** (June — shipped MCP server and MCPeval benchmark) → **July** (flagship journals).

- **Biomni** — *Science* ([10.1126/science.adz4351](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adz4351), Kexin Huang first, **Jure Leskovec** last). An action-discovery agent that mines tools, databases and protocols from thousands of publications across 25 domains.
- ***Nature* ran three** (PMID [42156546](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42156546/), [42310457](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42310457/), 42310463): a multi-agent system for automating scientific discovery, a framing paper defining autonomy levels for clinical agents, and a longitudinal disease-management conversational agent.
- ***Nature Machine Intelligence*, 07-01** — an agentic X-ray scientist that plans, executes, analyzes and iterates, **explicitly built on the Model Context Protocol** — the same substrate GCC2026 shipped in June.
- **Benchmarks** — a dedicated benchmark for AI scientists on omics discovery (PMID 42412809), Agentomics claiming to beat human-designed ML pipelines (42412783), and Pathology-CoT, a visual chain-of-thought agent trained on expert slide-navigation traces (42498734).

**The benchmarks are the maturity signal, not the *Science* paper.** A field gets evaluated when people begin to believe its claims. Six months from a conference floor to *Science*, and the protocol layer a tools community standardized in June is what the July papers are built on.

Four measurable entry points, across both solid and hematologic disease:

| PMID | Setting |
|---|---|

That is a structural advantage, and it suggests the interception playbook will be written in heme and exported to solid tumors — which is worth watching, because the corpus's solid-tumor vaults will see it late.

## How this was built

**Sources queried live**, no inferred endpoints:

- **PubMed E-utilities** — all 33 journals, July window, filtered `"journal article"[pt] NOT review[pt] NOT editorial[pt] NOT news[pt] NOT comment[pt] NOT "case reports"[pt]`. **1,795 research articles.**
- **OpenAlex** — author-scoped queries over the tracked-speaker roster; journal-level counts.
- **openFDA** Drugs@FDA; **ClinicalTrials.gov v2**; **`data/sources.yml`** for meetings ending in the window.

### The filter finding

**OpenAlex cannot separate research articles from front matter.** Its `type:review` returns ≈0 for these journals, so a type filter does nothing. Filtering on reference count is worse: it collapses *Blood* from 80 to 1 and *Cancer Discovery* from 29 to 3, because those publishers do not deposit reference lists — **a metadata artifact that silently deletes entire journals**. PubMed's hand-curated publication types are the only discriminator that behaves consistently across publishers. It also caught a bad number: OpenAlex reported 581 *Annals of Oncology* records for July; PubMed reports 22.

Two journals need OpenAlex as fallback: *Genome Research* and *Nature Machine Intelligence* return 1 record each from PubMed, which is an indexing gap, not real output.

### The method finding — the most important output of this pilot

| Route | Podium links found | Refutations found |
|---|---|---|
| Author-tracking (65-person roster) | **5** | **0** |
| Full-journal scan (1,795 papers) | 0 | **>12** |

→ `ev-2026-07-method-blind-spot`

**The mechanism is structural, not accidental.** People who publish benchmarks and negative results are not the people invited to give keynotes — partly *because* they are refuting the keynote speakers. An author-tracked corpus will therefore confirm its own priors indefinitely, and it will feel productive the whole time.

Concretely: the first pass of this briefing, built from author-tracking alone, titled its foundation-model section *"the clinical ones arrived"* and was **directionally wrong about the month's most important development on the corpus's most invested thread.**

**Both routes run monthly, with different jobs.** Author-tracking answers *who is advancing what*, at ~100 records/month. The full journal scan exists **to find refutations**, at 1,795 records/month scanned at title level only. Neither substitutes for the other.

### Cost

| Stage | Tokens |
|---|---|

### Gaps and unverified claims

1. **The "first CAR-T approved for solid tumors" approval is unresolved.** Highest priority.
2. **135 CT.gov result postings counted, not read.**
3. **OpenAlex author/institution metadata is wrong on at least 3 of 22 records checked** — two *Nature Cancer* papers and one *Nature Medicine* item resolved to single authors at implausible institutions ("The Nature Conservancy", "NatureServe", "Lear Inc."). Never trust `authorships` blindly.
4. The *NEJM* **daraxonrasib** record matched a correspondence letter, not the primary trial report.
5. **The 65-person roster was not fully resolved to OpenAlex IDs** — only 35 seed names were run. **Sara Mostafavi is on the roster and authored one of the seven refutation papers**; a complete run would have caught at least one.
6. `data/speakers.yml` reflects **what the vaults record**, not what happened at the meetings. Absence means "not yet extracted," never "did not speak."
7. **No news-source layer.** RSS is a rolling window and July had scrolled off. Historical months are reachable only through APIs — which is the argument for running monthly and persisting, rather than reconstructing on demand.
8. Journals are curated by **tier**, not coverage: *Cancer Research*, *Clinical Cancer Research*, *JNCI* and *Cancer Research Communications* are deliberately excluded (~138 papers/month).
